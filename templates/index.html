HwFar Community Guidelines

HwFar is a place for genuine conversations. Every account helps shape the tone of the community, so use it with care.

Be respectful

Speak to people as people. Do not harass, threaten, bully, discriminate against, or target someone because of who they are or what they believe.

Share quality content

Upload content you created or have permission to share. Keep images, videos, and voice notes relevant, authentic, and safe for the people receiving them. Do not upload shocking, exploitative, or deliberately misleading material.

Protect privacy

Do not publish another person’s private messages, photos, contact details, location, or recordings without their clear permission. Never pressure anyone to reveal personal information.

Do not misuse HwFar

No spam, scams, impersonation, phishing, malware, automated abuse, scraping, or attempts to disrupt the service. Do not use HwFar to sell illegal goods, coordinate harm, or evade the law.

Keep conversations safe

HwFar is not a place for sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, graphic violence, credible threats, or instructions for serious wrongdoing. If a conversation makes you feel unsafe, stop engaging and report it.

Help us protect the community

You can block or report accounts that break these rules. We may remove content, limit features, or suspend accounts that put people or the service at risk. Serious threats or illegal activity may be referred to the appropriate authorities.

One more thing

You must be at least 13 years old, and you are responsible for activity on your account. These guidelines may evolve as the community grows.
